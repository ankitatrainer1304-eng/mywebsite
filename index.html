Communication Tips

6 Rules That Transform
Every Conversation

Practical, proven techniques Ankita uses in every training — start applying them today.

01
⏸️

Master the Power Pause

A 2-second pause before answering signals confidence, gives you time to think, and commands attention in any room — boardroom or Zoom.

Presence
02
🎯

Lead With Your Message

Open with your key point first. Corporate audiences decide within 7 seconds whether to keep listening — don't make them wait for the point.

Clarity
03
🔊

Tone Speaks Louder

55% of communication is non-verbal. Match your vocal tone and pace to your message — warmth, authority, urgency are all set by how you say it.

Vocal Power
04
👂

Listen to Understand

Stop listening to reply — start listening to understand. Reflect back, ask one clarifying question, and let the other person feel truly heard.

Empathy
05
💡

The "So What" Test

Before every key point ask: "so what does this mean for my audience?" Tie every fact to a benefit — especially critical in sales conversations.

Sales Communication
06
🧍

Own Your Space

Plant your feet, open your posture, and hold eye contact 70% of the time. Your body language speaks before your first word lands.

Body Language
